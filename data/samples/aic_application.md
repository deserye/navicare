# Applying with AIC

The Agency for Integrated Care publishes public guidance on CareShield Life applications. That guidance covers how to qualify, how the scheme works, how to apply, which documents are needed, eFASS, and MOH-accredited severe-disability assessors.

AIC also explains applying on behalf of someone who lacks mental capacity. That path can require extra documents and proof of authority. Collect only what the official service asks for, and confirm consent before acting for another person.

NaviCare can help a caregiver prepare questions. It cannot submit an application.

Source to verify: https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/CareShield-Life
