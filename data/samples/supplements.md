# Supplements

The Ministry of Health explains that CareShield Life and ElderShield supplements add coverage on top of a basic plan. Supplement benefits differ by insurer and by plan. Use MOH’s comparison materials, then contact the insurer for a product-specific question.

This prototype does not recommend a supplement, rank insurers, or calculate a personal payout.

CPF Board’s scheme pages discuss MediSave payment, subsidies, premium support, and a premium-check service. Do not type an exact premium or account balance into NaviCare.

Source to verify: https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/careshield-life/careshield-life-and-eldershield-supplements/
