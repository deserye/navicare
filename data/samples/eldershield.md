# ElderShield

ElderShield is a closed long-term-care insurance scheme. CPF Board describes it as targeted at severe disability. Plan benefits depend on the historical scheme a person joined, so this note cannot say what a particular person receives.

Claim explanations refer to an inability to perform three of the six activities of daily living. The actual coverage check belongs in CPF member services, not in this prototype.

Do not treat ElderShield and CareShield Life as the same plan. One is the current scheme. The other is the closed historical scheme.

Source to verify: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/eldershield
