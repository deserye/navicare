# Activities of daily living

Public claim explanations for CareShield Life and ElderShield refer to six activities of daily living:

1. Washing
2. Dressing
3. Feeding
4. Toileting
5. Walking or moving around
6. Transferring

ElderShield explanations refer to inability to perform three of these six. An MOH-accredited severe-disability assessor is part of the official process. NaviCare cannot assess a person, read a medical report, or predict whether a claim will be approved.

Source to verify: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/careshield-life
