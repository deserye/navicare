# CareShield Life

CareShield Life is Singapore’s current long-term-care insurance scheme. CPF Board describes it as providing monthly cash payouts if an insured person develops severe disability.

CPF Board says Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents are eligible to join, and that the timing depends on birth year. It describes automatic coverage at age 30 for people born in 1980 or after. For many people born in 1979 or earlier, participation is described as optional, with exceptions that must be read on the official page.

Premiums can be paid with MediSave. Subsidies and premium support are described on the CPF Board page, together with a premium-check service. This note does not state a dollar amount. A personal premium or coverage result has to be checked in CPF member services.

Source to verify: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/careshield-life
